APPLICATION

1. PREP: Push back cuticles, buff surface, and wipe clean with an alcohol pad.

2. SIZE: Match the press-ons to your natural nails.

3. APPLY: Apply nail glue/sticky tab, press down at a 45-degree angle, and hold for 15 seconds.

REMOVAL

1. SOAK: Warm water + dish soap + cuticle oil for 10-15 mins.

2. LIFT: Gently use a wooden stick to lift edges. Do not force!

3. FINISH: Buff away residue and apply nourishing cuticle oil.
