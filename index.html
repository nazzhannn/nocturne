APPLICATION

STEP 1

Prep your natural nails by gently pushing back your cuticles, buffing the surface, and wiping them clean with an alcohol pad.

STEP 2

SIZE! Match the press-ons to your natural nails.

STEP 3

Apply a small amount of nail glue (or a sticky tab) to your natural nail, press the press-on down at a 45-degree angle, and hold firmly for 15 seconds. Repeat, and you're good to go!

REMOVAL

STEP 1

Soak your hands in a bowl of warm water mixed with a few drops of dish soap and cuticle oil for about 10 to 15 minutes.

STEP 2

Gently use a wooden cuticle stick to lift the edges of the press-ons. If they don't budge, soak for a few more minutes—never force them off!

STEP 3

Once they pop off, gently buff away any leftover residue and finish with some nourishing cuticle oil.
